Pawn Break

Your back rank is the gate. Open theirs before they open yours.

Rules
  • 8×8 board. White pawns start on rank 1, Black pawns on rank 8. White moves first.
  • Pawns move like chess pawns: one square forward, or two squares from their starting rank if both squares are empty. They capture one square diagonally forward.
  • No promotion, no en passant, no kings.
  • Win: move any pawn onto the opponent's back rank, or leave the opponent with no pawns.
  • Draw: a player who still has pawns but has no legal move.
